# Sample draft

**Example brief**

Topic: A five-minute Friday review for solo consultants. Audience: consultants juggling several client projects. Voice: direct and practical. Footage: desk, paper, pen, calendar. CTA: save the reel. Target: 32 seconds.

## Hooks

1. “Start Monday with one decision already made.”
2. “Before you close your laptop Friday, write these three lines.”
3. “Your Monday list starts with what you stop doing Friday.”

Use hook 2. Show a hand drawing three lines before the first sentence ends, with the words “Friday: 3 lines” on screen.

## Script and storyboard

| Time | Spoken line | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–6 sec | Before you close your laptop Friday, write these three lines. | Close-up of a pen drawing three lines | Friday: 3 lines |
| 6–14 sec | First, what did you finish? Name one thing a client can actually use. | Write “proposal sent” on line one | 1. Delivered |
| 14–22 sec | Second, what’s blocked? Write down who you’re waiting on and what you need. | Point to “budget approval” on line two | 2. Blocked |
| 22–32 sec | Third, what’s your first move Monday? Put it on the calendar before you sign off. Save this for Friday. | Block a slot for a follow-up | 3. First move |

## Caption

Before you sign off Friday, write down what you delivered, what’s blocked, and the first thing you’ll do Monday. Save this for your next Friday wrap-up.

## Review before filming

- Confirm the examples fit your audience.
- Use a fictional calendar and cover client names.
- Read the script aloud and adjust the last beat to fit.
